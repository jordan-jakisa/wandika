# Wandika privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026.

Wandika is a personal notes app for Mac, iPhone and Android, made by Jordan Mungujakisa. It has no server. Everything it reads stays on your device, apart from your notes, which go to the GitHub repository you choose.

## What Wandika reads, and why

| Source | What Wandika does with it |
|---|---|
| Your notes | Keeps them as Markdown files on your device. If you connect GitHub, it syncs them with the repository you pick. |
| GitHub (`repo` scope) | Lists your repositories so you can pick one, and syncs that one. |
| Gmail (`gmail.readonly`) | Shows recent messages in Today. Saves one as a note when you ask. It never sends, deletes or changes mail. |
| Google Calendar (`calendar.readonly`) | Shows today's events. Adds them to a note when you ask. |
| Google Tasks (`tasks`) | Shows tasks due today, and marks one done when you tick it. |
| Outlook mail and calendar | Same as Gmail and Google Calendar, read only. |
| Apple Calendar and Reminders | Read on the device, with your permission. Reminders are marked done when you tick them. |

## What Wandika does not do

- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
- No server: Wandika talks directly from your device to GitHub, Google, Microsoft and Apple.
- Your data is never sold, shared or used for anything other than the features above.
- Google user data is not used to train AI models.

## Where your sign-ins are kept

Sign-in tokens stay on your device: in the Keychain on Mac and iPhone, and in Wandika's private app storage on Android, which other apps can't read. To remove access, disconnect the account in Wandika's Settings. You can also revoke it in your Google, Microsoft or GitHub account settings.

Wandika's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
